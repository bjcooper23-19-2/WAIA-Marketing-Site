How pricing works

Agree the scope. Know the cost.

Tell us how many people need access and what support would help you get started. We'll recommend a sensible scope and provide a clear price before you commit.

Your proposal sets out the learner licences, subscription dates and total price in GBP, plus applicable taxes. These details are confirmed in the signed Customer Order Form.

Subscriptions have fixed start and end dates and do not renew automatically. The agreed subscription and any optional implementation are invoiced upfront, with payment due within 30 days unless otherwise agreed.

Optional implementation

Extra help, when you need it.

Implementation support is optional. If you need help beyond the standard subscription, we will agree the work and its price separately before you commit. Ongoing consultancy is not included.

Outside the standard scope

  • Bespoke policy drafting.
  • Custom learning content.
  • Managed programme ownership.
  • System integrations.
  • Unlimited advisory time.
