How pricing works

One scope, agreed in the Order Form.

Ask us for a price based on the learner licences you need.

The Order Form records the number of learner licences, the subscription start and end dates, and the total subscription price.

The signed Order Form confirms the amount in GBP, plus applicable taxes. WAIA does not renew automatically.

Optional implementation

Implementation, if agreed.

If implementation services are needed, their selection, scope and price are set out in the signed Customer Order Form. They do not create an entitlement to continuing consultancy.

Outside the standard scope

  • Bespoke policy drafting.
  • Custom learning content.
  • Managed programme ownership.
  • System integrations.
  • Unlimited advisory time.

Payment

One upfront invoice.

The full subscription fee and any agreed implementation fee appear on one upfront invoice. Payment is due within 30 days unless the signed Customer Order Form says otherwise.

Commercial FAQ

A few useful details before we talk.

Can we start with a smaller group?

We can discuss the number of learner licences you need. The agreed quantity will be in the signed Customer Order Form.

Is implementation required?

No. Implementation is optional and only provided where its scope is agreed in the signed Customer Order Form.

Are ongoing advisory services included?

No. The subscription does not include continuing consultancy or other services beyond any agreed implementation scope.

Does WAIA replace legal or compliance advice?

No. WAIA supports practical Guidance and evidence from real work, but it isn’t legal advice, regulatory assurance, a GRC platform or a financial ROI calculator.
