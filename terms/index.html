2. The WAIA service

WAIA is one integrated service that helps organisations establish workplace AI baselines, provide learning and guidance, and record evidence of how AI is used in work.

WAIA also records user-submitted workflow evidence, including frequency, net time change, checking and rework, outcomes and supporting context. It brings this evidence together for management review and, where supported, conservative indicative capacity estimates.

WAIA may include:

  • learner access
  • course content
  • manager learning pathways
  • organisation guidance display
  • guidance acknowledgement records
  • learner progress tracking
  • completion records
  • certificate or evidence records
  • organisation administration tools
  • reporting dashboards
  • support resources

The Customer Order Form sets the number of learner licences. The Customer may invite learners within that agreed quantity.

7. Learner records and evidence

WAIA may record learner access, progress, completion, assessment responses, guidance acknowledgements and certificate status.

Records may also include baseline responses, application evidence, organisation workflow grouping and administrator review decisions. Evidence strength and outcome direction are distinct: stronger evidence may support a positive, neutral, negative or mixed result, or remain unclear. Validated evidence requires accountable human review. Indicative capacity is not a financial saving, guaranteed productivity improvement or an employee performance score.

These records are intended to support internal visibility and evidence of learning and workplace application. They should not be treated as a complete measure of employee competence, compliance, performance or risk without wider organisational context.
