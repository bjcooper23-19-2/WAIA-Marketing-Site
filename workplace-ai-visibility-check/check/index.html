Step 1 of 4About 10–15 minutes

AI use and work

Think about what people do, not which licences the organisation owns.

If you asked team leads which AI tools people use in recurring work, how complete would the answer be?
Can you name the tasks or decisions that AI now affects?

Information and judgement

Use a real example if you can. An unclear answer is a useful finding.

For a task involving AI, do you know what information people enter into the tool?
When AI contributes to work that other people rely on, what happens before it is used?

Across teams

Notice whether useful practice is shared and whether managers hear about exceptions.

If two teams use AI for similar work, how would their approaches compare?
What could managers tell you about recent AI-assisted work?

Time and capacity

Finish by looking beyond a simple “time saved” claim.

When AI seems to save time, what happens to that capacity?

Choose what you can actually describe today. Your answers are not sent to WAIA or Tally.
