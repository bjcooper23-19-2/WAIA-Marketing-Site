You’re investing in workplace AI. WAIA helps organisations see where AI is being used, whether it is making work better, worse or unchanged, and what evidence management can rely on.

The three questions

Make workplace AI easier to judge.

Most AI dashboards show activity. WAIA is built around the management questions that decide whether AI is actually helping.

01

Where is AI being used?

Identify recurring workplace workflows and real application, not just licence access, training completion or survey responses.

02

Is it actually working?

Understand net impact, including checking, rework, negative outcomes and mixed results.

03

What can management rely on?

Separate isolated stories from stronger evidence and see where conclusions are ready for review.

Why WAIA

Usage is not value.

AI isn’t a strategy, it’s a tool. WAIA gives organisations the operating structure to understand what that tool is doing to recurring work.

Usage

A licence being opened doesn’t show whether work improved.

Training

Completing learning doesn’t show whether behaviour changed.

Anecdotes

One impressive example is different from repeated evidence.
