Cookie Notice

Cookies and browser storage used on the WAIA UK website and application.

Version 1.0 · Document issued 22 November 2026 · Provider: Nineteen Point Two Limited trading as WAIA

This notice explains how Nineteen Point Two Limited trading as WAIA uses cookies and similar browser technologies on the WAIA UK website, the Visibility Check and the WAIA application.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts and device identifiers.

These technologies can help a site work properly, remember preferences, support security, measure usage or support marketing activity.

3. Necessary authentication and service storage

Necessary cookies or browser storage are used where required for requested functions such as secure login, authentication, account access and platform security. WAIA application authentication sessions may be stored in browser localStorage.

Blocking necessary cookies or storage may prevent those features from working.

4. Analytics cookies

The UK WAIA marketing website uses a manually installed Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon for aggregate website statistics. Cloudflare describes this service as cookieless. WAIA UK loads the Cloudflare beacon manually unless an objection is saved. An analytics objection saved in this browser prevents our website script from loading the beacon on subsequent page loads. Our use of this technology remains subject to applicable UK privacy and electronic communications requirements.

For WAIA UK, use WAIA analytics preferences. When saved, the objection uses first-party localStorage on that domain, so it can apply on later visits from the same browser. The setting is specific to the waia.co.uk browser origin and does not automatically apply to app.waia.co.uk. Clearing browser storage removes the saved choice.

Google Search Console separately helps us understand search visibility. It does not require a Search Console browser tag on the WAIA site.

5. Source attribution, Visibility Check and forms

A valid source code in a direct WAIA link can be passed to a form without being stored in the browser. Marketing attribution does not use sessionStorage. The Visibility Check is a UK-site journey only. Its results are held in sessionStorage within the browser tab for that session; it is provided through WAIA UK.

Tally forms load after you choose to open them. Tally may use its own storage and access technologies when the form opens; its notice explains those technologies. WAIA has not enabled Tally's optional partial-submission saving or additional form tracking. Tally's own service and storage practices are described in its privacy information.

6. Managing browser storage

Use the WAIA UK analytics preferences page linked above to object to Cloudflare Web Analytics where enabled. You can also manage stored data and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the website or WAIA product works.

7. Changes to this Cookie Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website.
